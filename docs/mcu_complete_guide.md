## 🟣 The Infinity Saga (2008–2019)

### Phase One (2008–2012)

- [ ] **1. Iron Man** (2008) — 🎬 Film — 🟡
  > Billionaire Tony Stark builds a high-tech suit of armor to escape captivity and becomes Earth's first modern superhero.

- [ ] **2. The Incredible Hulk** (2008) — 🎬 Film — 🔴
  > Bruce Banner hunts for a cure to his gamma-powered condition while evading the military. Loosely connected to the wider MCU.

- [ ] **3. Iron Man 2** (2010) — 🎬 Film — 🔴
  > Tony Stark faces government pressure, a rival inventor, and the consequences of revealing his identity.

- [ ] **4. Thor** (2011) — 🎬 Film — 🔵
  > The arrogant God of Thunder is banished to Earth, where he learns humility and discovers his true heroic potential.

- [ ] **5. Captain America: The First Avenger** (2011) — 🎬 Film — 🟡
  > A frail soldier is transformed into a super-soldier during WWII to fight the villainous Hydra organization.

- [ ] **6. Marvel's The Avengers** (2012) — 🎬 Film — 🟢
  > Earth's mightiest heroes assemble for the first time to stop Loki and his alien army from conquering New York.

### Phase Two (2013–2015)

- [ ] **7. Iron Man 3** (2013) — 🎬 Film — 🔴
  > Tony Stark deals with PTSD from the Battle of New York while facing a mysterious terrorist known as the Mandarin.

- [ ] **8. Thor: The Dark World** (2013) — 🎬 Film — 🔴
  > Thor teams up with Loki to save the Nine Realms from an ancient race of dark elves seeking an Infinity Stone.

- [ ] **9. Captain America: The Winter Soldier** (2014) — 🎬 Film — 🟡
  > Steve Rogers uncovers a deep conspiracy within S.H.I.E.L.D. while confronting a deadly assassin from his past.

- [ ] **10. Guardians of the Galaxy** (2014) — 🎬 Film — 🔵
  > A ragtag group of intergalactic outlaws joins forces to stop a fanatic from destroying the galaxy with a powerful artifact.

- [ ] **11. Avengers: Age of Ultron** (2015) — 🎬 Film — 🟢
  > The Avengers battle a rogue AI created by Tony Stark that threatens all of humanity's existence.

- [ ] **12. Ant-Man** (2015) — 🎬 Film — 🔵
  > A master thief is recruited to wear a shrinking suit and pull off a high-stakes heist to save the world.

- [ ] **14. Doctor Strange** (2016) — 🎬 Film — 🟡

- [ ] **17. Thor: Ragnarok** (2017) — 🎬 Film — 🔵
  > Thor fights to save Asgard from his powerful sister Hela while trapped in a gladiatorial arena on a distant planet.

- [ ] **18. Black Panther** (2018) — 🎬 Film — 🟡
  > T'Challa returns home to Wakanda to claim the throne but faces a dangerous challenger who threatens his nation's future.

- [ ] **19. Avengers: Infinity War** (2018) — 🎬 Film — 🟢
  > The Avengers and allies race to stop Thanos from collecting all six Infinity Stones and wiping out half of all life.

- [ ] **20. Ant-Man and the Wasp** (2018) — 🎬 Film — 🔵
  > Scott Lang teams up with Hope van Dyne for an urgent mission into the Quantum Realm to rescue her mother.

- [ ] **21. Captain Marvel** (2019) — 🎬 Film — 🔵

- [ ] **23. Spider-Man: Far From Home** (2019) — 🎬 Film — 🔵

## 🎯 Quick-Reference: The Doomsday Fast Track

If you're short on time, here's your **bare minimum watchlist** in order:

| Priority | Title | Type | Why It Matters |
|----------|-------|------|----------------|
| 1 | **The Avengers** | Film | Establishes the original team dynamic |
| 2 | **Avengers: Infinity War** | Film | The Thanos saga — mandatory context |
| 3 | **Avengers: Endgame** | Film | Direct predecessor to Doomsday's story |
| 4 | **Loki** (S1 & S2) | Series | Explains the current state of the multiverse |
| 5 | **Spider-Man: No Way Home** | Film | The multiverse cracks open |
| 6 | **Doctor Strange: Multiverse of Madness** | Film | Multiversal incursions and dangers |
| 7 | **Deadpool & Wolverine** | Film | Bridges X-Men into the MCU |
| 8 | **The Falcon and the Winter Soldier** | Series | Sam Wilson becomes Captain America |
| 9 | **Thunderbolts*** | Film | The "New Avengers" team forms |
| 10 | **The Fantastic Four: First Steps** | Film | Introduces the team central to Doomsday |
| 11 | **Spider-Man: Brand New Day** | Film | Direct Doomsday setup |

> [!TIP]
> **Got more time?** Add these for richer context:
> - *Captain America: Civil War* — The Avengers' fracture point
> - *Shang-Chi* — Introduces a key Doomsday hero
> - *Black Panther: Wakanda Forever* — Sets up Wakanda's new leadership
> - *WandaVision* → *Multiverse of Madness* — The Scarlet Witch arc
> - *Captain America: Brave New World* — Sam's full Captain America story
> - *Daredevil: Born Again* — Wilson Fisk's political rise
